arXiv’s 2025 Annual Report is now available online! arXiv began publishing annual reports in 2020 to give our community a summary of arXiv’s initiatives, accomplishments, and financial activities each year. We also use our annual report as an opportunity to thank our members, sponsors, affiliates, individual donors, and arXiv enthusiasts – AKA, you!
